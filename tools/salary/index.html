Is your salary taxable in India?

Your pay lands in an Indian NRE account — so is it caught by Indian tax? For most seafarers the answer is no, and there's a CBDT circular that says so. Let's check yours against the four things that actually decide it.

▶ The myth we're about to clear up
A lot of officers believe money hitting an Indian bank account is "received in India" and therefore taxable. It isn't — the tax law taxes where your salary is earned and first received, not where it's later remitted. Crediting it to your NRE account is a transfer, not the taxing moment. Circular 13/2017 says this in as many words. This tool confirms whether you're inside that protection.
